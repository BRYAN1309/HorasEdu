# 🎓 HorasEdu – Digital Learning Platform for Batak Culture

HorasEdu is a **web-based Learning Management System (LMS)** built to preserve and promote **Batak culture and language** through an interactive, modern, and fun learning experience.
The platform combines **educational content, gamified learning, and chatbot assistance** to make cultural learning accessible and engaging in the digital era. 🌏

---

## 🚀 Features

### 📘 Interactive Learning Modules

Learn Batak language from **basic to advanced levels** with structured lessons and multimedia materials.

### 🧠 Quizzes & Final Exams

Evaluate learning progress through **interactive quizzes** and **final exams** that adapt to user performance.

### 🎮 Mini-Games

Engage with Batak culture through fun, gamified experiences:

* 🧩 Memory Cards
* ⌨️ Speed Typing
* 🔤 Drag & Match

### 💬 Chatbot Integration

Get **real-time guidance, feedback, and cultural insights** throughout the learning journey.

### 📊 User Progress Tracking

Automatically save quiz scores, track module completion, and revisit past lessons with ease.

## 🌍 Why We Built It

Batak culture and language are **gradually fading among younger generations**.
HorasEdu was created to **revitalize cultural learning** by transforming traditional education into an engaging digital experience.

Through technology, we aim to:

* Make learning Batak **fun and accessible**
* Promote **cultural identity and awareness**
* Preserve **heritage through innovation**

HorasEdu serves as a bridge between **tradition and technology**, empowering modern learners to reconnect with their cultural roots. ✨
